Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Najiha Closet ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide when registering — name, username, Gmail address, phone number, date of birth, and gender.
  • Order and delivery information — shipping address, phone number, and items purchased.
  • Messages you send us through the contact form or custom-size requests.
  • Basic usage data such as pages visited, to help us improve the site.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process and deliver your orders, and to communicate with you about them (including via WhatsApp, at the number you provide).
  • To respond to enquiries sent through the contact or custom-size forms.
  • To send updates about new arrivals or offers, only if you've subscribed to our newsletter — you can unsubscribe at any time.

How your information is stored

Account and order data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud). Access is restricted to authorized Najiha Closet staff. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Your choices

  • You can review or update your account details at any time by logging in.
  • You can request that we delete your account and associated data by contacting us.
  • You can opt out of marketing messages at any time.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, email us at hello@najihacloset.com or message us on WhatsApp.
