Our Story

Little clothes, made with a lot of care

Najiha Closet began as a small home-run tailoring service in Karachi, built around a simple idea: kidswear should feel as good as it looks — soft enough for play, and pretty enough for Eid. What started with a handful of custom orders has grown into a full collection spanning newborn essentials to party-ready pieces, all still finished with the same attention to fit and fabric.

Every custom order is measured, cut, and stitched by our small team, and every ready-made piece goes through the same quality checks before it reaches your door.
